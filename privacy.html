Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

This policy explains what happens to what you write in OneFlip. The short version: it stays on your device.

Your flips, questions, notes, tags and label pairs are stored on your iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, and nowhere else. OneFlip has no servers, so there is no place for your journal to be sent and no copy for us to hold. It is included in whatever backup you already use for your device, on Apple’s terms.

We do not track you. No analytics, no advertising, no attribution, no crash reporting, no third-party trackers. The one third-party component in the app is an animation renderer, and it has no access to the network.

Every flip is one bit from your device’s cryptographic random number generator — produced on the spot, and recorded nowhere but your own journal.

Permissions are requested only when you use the feature behind them. Microphone and speech recognition — to dictate a note; recognition runs on your device, and no audio or transcript leaves it. Photos — add-only, to save a share card you made; OneFlip never reads your library. Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode — to unlock your journal; iOS performs the check and returns only a yes or a no.

Reminders are scheduled and delivered by your device. Some of them quote your own question and the words you gave the two sides, so the reminder makes sense — which means that text can appear on your Lock Screen. Every kind of reminder can be turned off in Settings → Notifications, except the two that warn you before Pro ends; those carry no text of yours.

Widgets and watch complications are separate programs, so what they display is placed in a private container that only OneFlip and its own widgets can read. Your Apple Watch keeps its own copy of notes you write on it until it hands them to your iPhone. None of this leaves your devices.

Share cards, CSV files and PDF exports are created only when you ask for them. OneFlip hands the file to the destination you pick, and from that point that app’s privacy policy applies, not ours.

Signing in is optional — everything in OneFlip works without an account. It uses Sign in with Apple, so Apple decides what to share with us: your address, a private relay address, or nothing at all. Whatever it gives us stays on your device. Writing to support from inside the app pre-fills your app version, device model, iOS version, plan, whether you signed in, and — if you wrote from an error screen — the code of that error. Never anything from your journal, and you can edit the message before sending.

Sign in with Apple also gives the app an identifier for you — a string that means nothing outside OneFlip. It stays on your device, and the app uses it to ask iOS one question: is this sign-in still valid? If you remove OneFlip in Settings → your Apple Account → Sign in with Apple, the app signs you out on its own the next time you open it.

OneFlip Pro is sold by Apple. Payment, renewal and refunds are handled by the App Store, and we never see your payment details. The app asks iOS one yes-or-no question: does this Apple ID own Pro?

To erase everything, delete the app: that removes the whole app, its settings and the watch app with it. If you signed in, Settings → Account → Delete account & all data erases your journal, your stats, your flip counts and your account from this device and signs you out. App settings stay — except the journal lock, which switches off, since there is nothing left to lock. Words you saved on your Apple Watch clear the next time it syncs with your iPhone. We hold no copy, so there is nothing to request from us. OneFlip is not directed at children under 13. Changes to this policy ship with app updates, and the date above changes with them. Questions: oneflip.app@gmail.com.
